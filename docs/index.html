Beam

Send anything between your devices.

Photos, videos, files, folders, text and links go straight from one device to another, encrypted the whole way. Like AirDrop, but on Android, Windows and Mac too.

Downloads

Each app keeps itself up to date after you install it. You'll need a Beam account; ask your Beam admin for an invite code.

Android

Runs in the background, shows up in the share sheet, and sends phone to phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi when there's no internet.

Android 10 or newer

How to install
  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, open Beam and sign in.
  4. Allow notifications so Beam can ask before accepting files.
Download for Android

Windows

Lives in the system tray, starts with Windows, and adds Beam to the Share window and the right-click menu. Converts iPhone photos and videos so they open on your PC.

Windows 10 or 11

How to install
  1. Download Beam.exe and open it.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Beam installs itself and opens. Sign in.
  4. To add Beam to the Share window, click Yes when Windows asks for permission once.
Download for Windows

Mac

Lives in the menu bar and stays reachable in the background. Adds "Send with Beam" to Finder's right-click menu.

macOS 13 or newer

How to install
  1. Download Beam-mac.zip and open it to unzip.
  2. Drag Beam into your Applications folder.
  3. Right-click Beam and choose Open. If macOS still blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. Sign in. Beam appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen.
Download for Mac

iPhone, iPad and everything else

Use Beam in the browser: no install needed. Ask your Beam admin for the web address, open it in Safari, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen to keep it like an app.

Private by design

Files go directly between devices over an encrypted connection. Beam's server only introduces devices and never sees what you send.

Any size, any amount

Send huge videos, whole folders or thousands of photos at once. If the connection drops, Beam picks up where it left off.

Only your people

Beam is invite-only. Your own devices find each other anywhere, and nothing arrives without your OK unless it's from your own devices.

Beam is a private project. Downloads come straight from this repository's releases.
